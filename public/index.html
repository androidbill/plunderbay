How to play

  • Hide your fleet. Place 5 ships and 3 treasure chests on a 10×10 sea. Ships can sit side by side, but they can't overlap.
  • Take turns firing. Pick a square on the enemy waters and hit FIRE. You see a splash 💦 for a miss and fire 🔥 for a hit.
  • Treasure! 💰 Hitting a chest loots it and you fire again.
  • Sink every ship in the enemy fleet to win. A ship sinks when all its squares are hit.
  • 2v2 mode: each crew of two shares one fleet and one set of shots. Turns go Red → Blue → Red → Blue, so teammates alternate firing.
